# Diabetes-App
Diabetes mellitus is a chronic metabolic disorder characterised by persistently elevated blood glucose levels resulting from defects in insulin secretion, insulin action, or both. As of 2024, over 500 million adults globally are living with diabetes, with prevalence projected to reach 700 million by 2045. In Pakistan, the burden is disproportionately high — approximately 30% of adults are affected, placing the country among the world’s top ten diabetic populations. Early and accurate detection is critical: undiagnosed or poorly managed diabetes causes progressive damage to the kidneys, eyes, nerves, heart, and blood vessels, sub stantially increasing morbidity and mortality. Conventional clinical screening is resource intensive and often unavailable in underserved regions, creating a compelling case for AI-assisted decision support tools. This project develops a full-stack machine learning application that predicts diabetes risk using the Pima Indians Diabetes Database. Two complementary models are built and deployed in parallel: • AnArtificial Neural Network (ANN) — optimised for predictive accuracy using a feed-forward multi-layer perceptron with ReLU activation and Sigmoid output. • AnAdaptive Neuro-Fuzzy Inference System (ANFIS) — a hybrid neuro-fuzzy model that maps input features through learnable Gaussian membership functions to produce human-interpretable IF–THEN rules. To address the explainability gap common in black-box AI models, SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) is applied to the ANN to quantify each feature’s contribution to individual predictions.
